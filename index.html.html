Data privacy & map

Lead and buyer records are stored in local browser storage. Map tiles and geocoding are supplied by third-party services when you use the map. Avoid entering sensitive personal information you do not need.

This is a standalone prototype, not a hosted multi-user system. It does not include user accounts, cloud sync, automated SMS/email, MLS integration, or legal compliance review.
